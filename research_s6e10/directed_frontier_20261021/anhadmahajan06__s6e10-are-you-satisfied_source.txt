This notebook documents **Version 28.0** of our end-to-end training pipeline for Kaggle Playground Series s6e10. The objective is binary classification predicting passenger satisfaction (`satisfaction`: 0 or 1), evaluated strictly by Area Under the ROC Curve (ROC AUC).

### Key Architectural Upgrades in Version 28.0

1. **The 3-Way Clean Original Route-Persona Prior (`orig_route_3way_prior`):**
   - In Version 27, extracting Bayesian smoothed priors from the clean 129k original dataset for `[Flight Distance, Class]` yielded a major breakthrough to `0.96100` on the Public Leaderboard.
   - In Version 28.0, we expand this to the ultimate aviation micro-segment: the 3-way compound key **`[Flight Distance, Class, Type of Travel]`** from the clean 129k original records (99.97% test overlap, zero synthetic label-flipping noise).
   - **Empirical Validation:** Scored an astonishing **`0.83968` standalone AUC** on competition training labels (outperforming the V27 prior by **+0.0197 AUC**)!

2. **Expanded 88 Sovereign Feature Suite (+3 High-Impact Formulations):**
   - `orig_route_3way_prior`: Clean original 3-way Route × Class × Travel Purpose prior (`0.83968` AUC).
   - `Clean_Food_Ratio`: Ratio of cabin hygiene to catering service `(Cleanliness + 1.0) / (Food and drink + 1.0)`.
   - `Wifi_Booking_Gap`: Subjective digital delivery disparity between pre-flight booking and in-flight connectivity `Inflight wifi - Ease of Online booking`.
   - Total: **78 static features + 10 in-fold target encodings = Exactly 88 Sovereign Features**.

3. **Dual-Seeded ExtraTrees Twin (`LightGBM_ExtraTrees_Twin`):**
   - In Version 27, `LightGBM_ExtraTrees` was assigned **30.0% weight** by Caruana, driving our public score to `0.96100`.
   - In Version 28.0, we upgrade ExtraTrees into a **Dual-Seed Twin** (Seed 42 + Seed 2026).
   - Because ExtraTrees randomizes split thresholds, averaging two independent random seeds cuts Monte Carlo split variance by **29.3%**, stabilizing its predictions and boosting its fidelity even further.

4. **Unified Semi-Supervised Candidate Pool for Ensembling:**
   - The Stage 2 pseudo-refined models (`XGBoost_Depth8_Wide_Pseudo` and `XGBoost_Tuned_D9_Pseudo`, trained on 8,379 confident test predictions with `sample_weight=0.50`) are now directly injected into the candidate pool for Caruana Forward Selection and SLSQP Simplex Optimization.
   - The ensembling optimizers can now dynamically blend the pure base models with semi-supervised pseudo models, capturing test-set boundary adaptations.

5. **Multi-Track Synergy Auto-Discovery:**
   - Detects mounted top historical submissions (`0.96100`, `0.96097`, `0.96096`, `0.96095`) and automatically exports `submission_track1_plus_meta.csv` targeting **0.96115+**!

### CELL 8 markdown
## 5. Feature Engineering: 88 Sovereign Features Suite

We formulate **88 high-impact features** across five specialized tiers:
1. **Physical Flight Dynamics & Delay Structure:** Total delay, delay disparity, delay ratio, and delay per mile.
2. **Customer Satisfaction Polarity & Non-Zero Engagement:** `Net_Satisfaction_Score`, `NonZero_Rating_Mean`, `NonZero_Rating_Std`, and `NonZero_Ratings_Ratio` (isolating true ratings from "Not Applicable" `0`s).
3. **Route Profile Aggregations & Relative Perceptions:** Route-level expectation means, delay volatility (`route_std_delay`), and subjective perception deviations (`rel_wifi_route`, `rel_seat_route`, `rel_delay_route`).
4. **Psychological Ergonomics & Digital Interaction Gaps:** `Clean_Food_Ratio`, `Wifi_Booking_Gap`, `Digital_Service_Index`, `Digital_Satisfaction_Gap`, `Physical_Service_Index`, `Cabin_Comfort_Cluster`, `Service_Expectation_Gap`, `Wifi_Boarding_Ratio`.
5. **Quadruple Clean Original Teacher Knowledge:** Bayesian smoothed route prior, Route-Class prior (+0.09 AUC), Route-Travel prior (+0.076 AUC), **3-Way Route × Class × Travel Purpose prior (`0.83968` standalone AUC!)**, plus XGBoost and LightGBM prior probabilities and logits trained strictly on clean original records.
6. **Zero-Leakage In-Fold Target Encodings:** 10 compound interaction keys with inner 5-fold CV to prevent self-leakage.

# ==============================================================================
# 5.1 FEATURE ENGINEERING: DOMAIN INTERACTIONS, POLARITY, NON-ZERO SURVEY & ROUTE PROFILES
# ==============================================================================
full_df = pd.concat([train_raw.drop(columns=["id", TARGET], errors="ignore"), test_raw.drop(columns=["id"], errors="ignore")], axis=0).reset_index(drop=True)

CATS = ["Gender", "Customer Type", "Type of Travel", "Class"]
NUMS = ["Age", FD, "Departure Delay in Minutes", "Arrival Delay in Minutes"]
RATINGS = [
    "Inflight wifi service", "Departure/Arrival time convenient", "Ease of Online booking",
    "Gate location", "Food and drink", "Online boarding", "Seat comfort",
    "Inflight entertainment", "On-board service", "Leg room service",
    "Baggage handling", "Checkin service", "Cleanliness"
]
CORE_FEATS = NUMS + RATINGS + CATS

# 1. Missing Value Imputation
full_df["Arrival Delay in Minutes"] = full_df["Arrival Delay in Minutes"].fillna(full_df["Departure Delay in Minutes"]).fillna(0.0)

# 2. Delay Dynamics & Survey Engagement
full_df["Delay_Total"] = (full_df["Departure Delay in Minutes"] + full_df["Arrival Delay in Minutes"]).astype(np.float32)
full_df["Delay_Diff"]  = (full_df["Arrival Delay in Minutes"] - full_df["Departure Delay in Minutes"]).astype(np.float32)
full_df["Delay_Ratio"] = ((full_df["Arrival Delay in Minutes"] + 1.0) / (full_df["Departure Delay in Minutes"] + 1.0)).astype(np.float32)
full_df["Delay_Per_Mile"] = (full_df["Delay_Total"] / (full_df[FD] + 1.0)).astype(np.float32)
full_df["Zero_Ratings_Count"] = (full_df[RATINGS] == 0).sum(axis=1).astype(np.float32)

# 3. Customer Satisfaction Polarity Index (Psychological Delight vs Frustration)
high_ratings = (full_df[RATINGS] >= 4).sum(axis=1).astype(np.float32)
low_ratings  = ((full_df[RATINGS] <= 2) & (full_df[RATINGS] > 0)).sum(axis=1).astype(np.float32)
full_df["Net_Satisfaction_Score"] = ((high_ratings - low_ratings) / (13.0 - full_df["Zero_Ratings_Count"] + 1.0)).astype(np.float32)

# 4. Non-Zero Survey Engagement & Service Consistency (Handling '0' = Not Applicable)
rating_mat = full_df[RATINGS].values
mask_nonzero = (rating_mat > 0)
nonzero_counts = mask_nonzero.sum(axis=1).astype(np.float32)

rating_sums = rating_mat.sum(axis=1).astype(np.float32)
nonzero_mean = np.zeros(len(full_df), dtype=np.float32)
valid_mask = (nonzero_counts > 0)
nonzero_mean[valid_mask] = rating_sums[valid_mask] / nonzero_counts[valid_mask]
full_df["NonZero_Rating_Mean"] = nonzero_mean

# Non-zero standard deviation (perception volatility across experienced services)
rating_nan = np.where(rating_mat > 0, rating_mat, np.nan)
nonzero_std_vec = np.nanstd(rating_nan, axis=1)
full_df["NonZero_Rating_Std"] = np.nan_to_num(nonzero_std_vec, nan=0.0).astype(np.float32)
full_df["NonZero_Ratings_Ratio"] = (nonzero_counts / 13.0).astype(np.float32)

# 5. Missing Digital Service Indicators
full_df["Wifi_is_zero"] = (full_df["Inflight wifi service"] == 0).astype(np.float32)
full_df["Boarding_is_zero"] = (full_df["Online boarding"] == 0).astype(np.float32)

# 6. Psychological Ergonomics & Digital Interaction Gaps (NEW in V28)
full_df["Clean_Food_Ratio"] = ((full_df["Cleanliness"] + 1.0) / (full_df["Food and drink"] + 1.0)).astype(np.float32)
full_df["Wifi_Booking_Gap"] = (full_df["Inflight wifi service"] - full_df["Ease of Online booking"]).astype(np.float32)

# 7. Flight Context & Service Interactions
full_df["Digital_Service_Index"] = ((full_df["Inflight wifi service"] + 1.0) * (full_df["Online boarding"] + 1.0)).astype(np.float32)
full_df["Digital_Satisfaction_Gap"] = (full_df["Inflight wifi service"] - full_df["Online boarding"]).astype(np.float32)
full_df["Physical_Service_Index"] = ((full_df["Seat comfort"] + full_df["Cleanliness"] + full_df["Food and drink"]) / 3.0).astype(np.float32)
full_df["Cabin_Comfort_Cluster"] = ((full_df["Seat comfort"] + full_df["Leg room service"] + full_df["Cleanliness"]) / 3.0).astype(np.float32)

class_map = {"Eco": 0, "Eco Plus": 1, "Business": 2}
class_numeric = full_df["Class"].map(class_map).fillna(0).astype(np.float32)
full_df["Service_Expectation_Gap"] = (full_df["Physical_Service_Index"] - (class_numeric * 1.5)).astype(np.float32)
full_df["Wifi_Boarding_Ratio"] = ((full_df["Inflight wifi service"] + 0.5) / (full_df["Online boarding"] + 0.5)).astype(np.float32)

loyalty_num = (full_df["Customer Type"] == "Loyal Customer").astype(np.float32)
biz_num = (full_df["Type of Travel"] == "Business travel").astype(np.float32)
full_df["Loyal_Business_Segment"] = (loyalty_num * 2.0 + biz_num).astype(np.float32)

# 8. Frequency Encodings
CNT_FEATS = pd.DataFrame(index=full_df.index)
for col in NUMS:
    CNT_FEATS[f"cnt_{col}"] = full_df[col].map(train_raw[col].value_counts()).fillna(0).astype(np.float32)

# 9. Route Profile Means & Volatility (Grouped by Flight Distance)
coded_full = full_df[CORE_FEATS].copy()
for c in CATS:
    coded_full[c] = coded_full[c].astype("category").cat.codes

route_group = coded_full.groupby(FD)
ROUTE_PROFILES = pd.DataFrame(index=full_df.index)
for c in CORE_FEATS:
    if c != FD:
        ROUTE_PROFILES[f"route_mean_{c}"] = route_group[c].transform("mean").astype(np.float32)
ROUTE_PROFILES["route_size"] = route_group["Age"].transform("size").astype(np.float32)
ROUTE_PROFILES["route_std_delay"] = route_group["Arrival Delay in Minutes"].transform("std").fillna(0.0).astype(np.float32)

# 10. Relative Route Perception Deviations (Subjective vs Route Expectation)
full_df["rel_wifi_route"]  = (full_df["Inflight wifi service"] - ROUTE_PROFILES["route_mean_Inflight wifi service"]).astype(np.float32)
full_df["rel_seat_route"]  = (full_df["Seat comfort"] - ROUTE_PROFILES["route_mean_Seat comfort"]).astype(np.float32)
full_df["rel_delay_route"] = (full_df["Arrival Delay in Minutes"] - ROUTE_PROFILES["route_mean_Arrival Delay in Minutes"]).astype(np.float32)

print(f"[OK] Route Profile Means & Volatility Built: {ROUTE_PROFILES.shape[1]} features")
print(f"[OK] Frequency Counts Built: {CNT_FEATS.shape[1]} features")
print(f"[OK] Context, Perception & Psychological Ergonomic Features Formulated: 24 features")

stats_class = orig_df_keys.groupby("key_fd_class")["y"].agg(["sum", "count"])
smoothed_orig_class = (stats_class["sum"] + orig_global_prior * 15.0) / (stats_class["count"] + 15.0)

stats_travel = orig_df_keys.groupby("key_fd_travel")["y"].agg(["sum", "count"])

# C. Breakthrough 3-Way Route x Class x Travel Purpose Prior (0.83968 Standalone AUC!)
orig_df_keys["key_3way"] = orig_df_keys["fd"].astype(str) + "_" + orig_df_keys["class"].astype(str) + "_" + orig_df_keys["travel"].astype(str)

def combine_keys(df, cols):
    code = np.zeros(len(df), dtype=np.int64)
    for c in cols:
        k = pd.factorize(df[c], use_na_sentinel=False)[0]
        code = code * (k.max() + 1) + k
    return pd.factorize(code)[0]

def apply_bayesian_te(trn_keys, y_tr, eval_keys_list, weight=25.0):
    global_prior = float(y_tr.mean())
    fit_encodings = {}
    eval_encodings_list = [{} for _ in eval_keys_list]
    
    inner_cv = KFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=SEED)
    for col in trn_keys.columns:
        k_fit = trn_keys[col].values
        oof_te = np.zeros(len(trn_keys), dtype=np.float32)
        
        # Inner CV for training fold OOF (prevents self-leakage)
        for in_trn, in_val in inner_cv.split(k_fit):
            stats = pd.DataFrame({"k": k_fit[in_trn], "y": y_tr[in_trn]}).groupby("k")["y"].agg(["sum", "count"])
            smoothed = (stats["sum"] + global_prior * weight) / (stats["count"] + weight)
            oof_te[in_val] = pd.Series(k_fit[in_val]).map(smoothed).fillna(global_prior).values.astype(np.float32)
        fit_encodings[f"te_{col}"] = oof_te
        
        # Fit on outer training fold; apply strictly to validation and test slices
        stats = pd.DataFrame({"k": k_fit, "y": y_tr}).groupby("k")["y"].agg(["sum", "count"])
        smoothed = (stats["sum"] + global_prior * weight) / (stats["count"] + weight)
        for idx, ev_keys in enumerate(eval_keys_list):
            k_eval = ev_keys[col].values
            eval_encodings_list[idx][f"te_{col}"] = pd.Series(k_eval).map(smoothed).fillna(global_prior).values.astype(np.float32)
            
    return pd.DataFrame(fit_encodings), [pd.DataFrame(d) for d in eval_encodings_list]

def train_cv_model(model_name, fit_fn):
    print(f"\n{'='*20} Training: {model_name} ({N_FOLDS} Folds) {'='*20}")
    oof = np.zeros(NTR, dtype=np.float32)
    test_accum = np.zeros(NTE, dtype=np.float32)
    t0 = time.time()
    
    for fold, (trn_idx, val_idx) in enumerate(FOLDS):
        X_tr, X_va, X_te, y_tr, y_va = FOLD_MATRICES[fold]
            
        predict_fn = fit_fn(X_tr, y_tr, X_va, y_va, fold)
        oof[val_idx] = predict_fn(X_va)
        test_accum += predict_fn(X_te) / len(FOLDS)
        
        fold_auc = roc_auc_score(y_va, oof[val_idx])
        print(f"   Fold {fold + 1:2d}/{N_FOLDS} AUC: {fold_auc:.6f}")
        
    overall_auc = roc_auc_score(y_train, oof)
    print(f"[AUC] {model_name} Overall OOF AUC: {overall_auc:.6f} ({time.time() - t0:.1f}s)")
    OOF_PREDS[model_name] = oof
    TEST_PREDS[model_name] = test_accum
    gc.collect()
    if GPU: torch.cuda.empty_cache()

## 8. Stage 2: Calibrated Sample-Weighted Pseudo-Labeling Refinement

We extract high-confidence test observations ($p \ge 0.985$ or $p \le 0.015$, >99.7% estimated precision) from the Stage 1 consensus.

- In Version 28.0, the resulting pseudo-refined models (`XGBoost_Depth8_Wide_Pseudo` and `XGBoost_Tuned_D9_Pseudo`) are directly ingested into the unified ensembling pool, allowing Caruana and SLSQP to freely combine pure base models with semi-supervised models.

            oof_p[val_idx] = pred_fn(X_va)
            test_accum_p += pred_fn(X_te) / len(FOLDS)
            
        auc_p = roc_auc_score(y_train, oof_p)
        print(f"   * {m_name:28s} OOF AUC: {auc_p:.6f}")
        PSEUDO_OOF[m_name] = oof_p
        PSEUDO_TEST[m_name] = test_accum_p
        
        # Inject pseudo-refined models directly into the ensembling candidate pool!
        OOF_PREDS[m_name] = oof_p
        TEST_PREDS[m_name] = test_accum_p
        
    print(f"[OK] Weighted Pseudo-Refinement Completed in {time.time() - t_pseudo_start:.1f}s.")
    print(f"[OK] Ingested Pseudo-Refined Models into Ensembling Pool (Total Models: {len(OOF_PREDS)})")

## 9. Stage 3: Multi-Strategy Ensembling & Dual-Domain Logit Optimization

We evaluate continuous simplex ensembling across both linear probability space and unconstrained logit space with temperature scaling:
1. **Continuous Linear SLSQP Simplex Champion:** Optimal convex weights $\mathbf{w}$ on the probability simplex.
2. **Temperature-Scaled Logit SLSQP Champion ($\tau = 0.95$):** Optimization in log-odds space, sharpening margin contrast.
3. **Discrete Caruana Forward Selection:** Greedy hill climbing across the unified candidate pool (Base Titans + Semi-Supervised Twins).
4. **Pure Double-Twin XGBoost Trio:** 45% Wide Twin + 45% D9 Twin + 10% Depth 10 Anchor.
5. **The Apex Consensus Blend:** 50% Logit SLSQP + 50% Linear Champion.

# ----------------- 4. CONTINUOUS LINEAR SLSQP SIMPLEX OPTIMIZATION -----------------
print(f"[*] Optimizing Continuous Linear ROC AUC Weights via SLSQP Simplex Solver ({n_models} Models)...")
t_slsqp = time.time()

def linear_slsqp_objective(weights):
    w = np.array(weights)
    w = w / np.sum(w)
    blend = sum(w[i] * OOF_PREDS[model_names[i]] for i in range(n_models))
    return -roc_auc_score(y_train, blend)

initial_weights = [1.0 / n_models] * n_models
bounds = [(0.0, 1.0)] * n_models
constraint = {'type': 'eq', 'fun': lambda w: np.sum(w) - 1.0}

opt_res_lin = minimize(
    linear_slsqp_objective,
    initial_weights,
    method='SLSQP',
    bounds=bounds,
    constraints=constraint,
    options={'eps': 1e-3, 'maxiter': 50}
)

slsqp_weights_lin = opt_res_lin.x / np.sum(opt_res_lin.x)
oof_slsqp = sum(slsqp_weights_lin[i] * OOF_PREDS[model_names[i]] for i in range(n_models))
te_slsqp  = sum(slsqp_weights_lin[i] * TEST_PREDS[model_names[i]] for i in range(n_models))
auc_slsqp = roc_auc_score(y_train, oof_slsqp)
print(f"[OK] Linear SLSQP Simplex Completed in {time.time() - t_slsqp:.1f}s (AUC: {auc_slsqp:.6f}).")

# ----------------- 5. CONTINUOUS LOGIT SLSQP OPTIMIZATION (TAU = 0.95) -----------------
print(f"[*] Optimizing Temperature-Scaled Logit SLSQP Simplex (tau=0.95, {n_models} Models)...")
t_logit_opt = time.time()
TAU = 0.95

def logit_slsqp_objective(weights):
    w = np.array(weights)
    w = w / np.sum(w)
    blend_z = sum(w[i] * OOF_LOGITS[model_names[i]] for i in range(n_models)) / TAU
    return -roc_auc_score(y_train, expit(blend_z))

opt_res_log = minimize(
    logit_slsqp_objective,
    slsqp_weights_lin.copy(),
    method='SLSQP',
    bounds=bounds,
    constraints=constraint,
    options={'eps': 1e-3, 'maxiter': 50}
)

slsqp_weights_log = opt_res_log.x / np.sum(opt_res_log.x)
oof_logit_z = sum(slsqp_weights_log[i] * OOF_LOGITS[model_names[i]] for i in range(n_models)) / TAU
oof_logit = expit(oof_logit_z)
te_logit_z  = sum(slsqp_weights_log[i] * TEST_LOGITS[model_names[i]] for i in range(n_models)) / TAU
te_logit = expit(te_logit_z)
auc_logit = roc_auc_score(y_train, oof_logit)
print(f"[OK] Logit SLSQP Simplex Completed in {time.time() - t_logit_opt:.1f}s (AUC: {auc_logit:.6f}).")

# ----------------- 6. CARUANA FORWARD SELECTION (50 ITERS ACROSS ALL CANDIDATES) -----------------
def caruana_forward_auc(y_true, pred_dict, max_iters=50):
    names = list(pred_dict.keys())
    weights = {m: 0 for m in names}
    current_blend = np.zeros(len(y_true), dtype=np.float64)
    best_auc = 0.0
    
    for it in range(max_iters):
        best_cand = None
        best_cand_auc = -1.0
        for m in names:
            cand_blend = (current_blend * it + pred_dict[m]) / (it + 1)
            auc = roc_auc_score(y_true, cand_blend)
            if auc > best_cand_auc:
                best_cand_auc = auc
                best_cand = m
        current_blend = (current_blend * it + pred_dict[best_cand]) / (it + 1)
        weights[best_cand] += 1
        best_auc = best_cand_auc
        
    normalized_weights = {m: weights[m] / max_iters for m in names}
    return normalized_weights, current_blend, best_auc

caruana_weights, oof_caruana, auc_caruana = caruana_forward_auc(y_train, OOF_PREDS, max_iters=50)
te_caruana = np.zeros(NTE, dtype=np.float64)
