# S6E10 | 0.96188 LB | a stack of stacks inflates nested CV (family-wise L2 + a LightGBM blend)

A trimmed copy of Dariush Afshar's *every public OOF pair I could check, in one logistic stack*
(LB 0.96187). His loaders and his checks (alignment, scale, near-copies) are unchanged. The pool has
**248 members**: his 247 plus `gpx_stack245`, which goodpjw2008's extra-members dataset now holds next to
his single models. That 248th "member" is goodpjw2008's whole nested 245-member stack (OOF AUC
0.962224), not a model, and almost all of its members are already in the pool. Version 7 excluded it;
this version keeps it on purpose, to measure what a stack of stacks does here. His comparison stacks
(C = 1, the 135-member base pool), the coefficient tables and the demonstration of section 6 are removed.

> **TL;DR**
> - With `gpx_stack245` in the pool, nested CV keeps rising as the L2 penalty weakens (family C = 0.003 →
>   0.011: blend +19 → +48 × 1e-6) and never turns, while on the clean 247 members it peaks at C = 0.003.
> - Rebuilt **fully nested** (the stage-1 stack built from the training folds only), the same two-stage
>   stack is no better than one stage and gets worse as C grows. The whole gain was **nested-CV
>   optimism**, up to +4 × 1e-5 at C = 0.011.
> - Use the clean 247-member blend (version 7). Do not stack a published stack's OOF.

On top of his pool, this notebook tries two stacker changes and picks among them by nested CV:
- **family-wise L2** in the logistic regression, from goodpjw2008's
  [Part 11](https://www.kaggle.com/code/goodpjw2008/s6e10-228-member-meta-stack-lb-0-96184)
  ("regularize families, not members"): TabPFN columns × 3, other foundation models × 1.5, which divides
  their effective L2 penalty by 9 and 2.25. He fixed C = 0.003 on his 245 members; I re-measured it in
  [an earlier notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/ewtherka/s6e10-stacker-s-c-penalty-on-model-families)
  with a converged solver, and here I search C together with it on the full public pool;
- *Trust the CV*'s **0.8 LR + 0.2 LightGBM** blend (both standardised; LightGBM parameters and input
  families as there).

Settings: plain LR and blend, each with flat C = 0.003 and family C ∈ {0.003, 0.004, 0.005, 0.006}, plus
an extended sweep to C = 0.011 in a separate run. The baseline is Dariush Afshar's recipe, **plain LR with
flat C = 0.003**.

1. Nested CV on Dariush Afshar's own meta folds, `StratifiedKFold(5, shuffle=True, random_state=42)`
   (the folds most members were trained on), fold-mean AUC. On his 247 members this baseline reproduces
   his stack (0.962211 against 0.962212, the mean of his five published fold AUCs; his headline 0.962205
   is the pooled AUC).
2. **Cross-fitted choice**: for each fold, the setting is picked on the other four and scored on that one.
3. The chosen setting is re-run against the baseline on two untouched meta-fold seeds (7, 101); it is
   adopted only if it beats the baseline on seed 42 and on both of them.
4. Refit on every training row; `submission.csv` and the nested OOF of the adopted stack are written.

CPU only, no internet, about 80 minutes on Kaggle.

## The decisive test: a stack of stacks, fully nested

`gpx_stack245` was fitted on every training row, including the fold being scored, so a nested CV over it
cannot say whether the gain is a real two-stage effect or optimism. I rebuilt the idea so that it can
([notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/ewtherka/is-it-reliable?scriptVersionId=357862898)): for every outer fold (seed 42), a plain LR stack of the 247
members (flat C = 0.003, goodpjw2008's recipe) is built **from the four training folds only** (inner
nested CV, seed 7, for its OOF there; a fit on all four scores the held-out fold, as a test set would be
scored). Stage 2 is family-wise LR on [247 members + that stack], and the LightGBM stacker also gets the
stage-1 column. The held-out fold never touches stage 1. The single-stage arms reproduce version 7
exactly (0.962211 and 0.962228; fold 1, 0.962358, matches Dariush Afshar's to the last digit).

| stage 2 | C | fully nested | Δ vs one stage (SE, folds up) | with `gpx_stack245` | inflation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| plain LR, family | 0.003 | 0.962212 | +1.0 (5.7, 3/5) | 0.962213 | +1 |
| plain LR, family | 0.006 | 0.962205 | −6.0 (6.3, 2/5) | 0.962220 | +15 |
| plain LR, family | 0.011 | 0.962200 | −11.3 (6.4, 1/5) | 0.962241 | +41 |
| blend, family | 0.003 | 0.962221 | −6.2 (3.0, 1/5) | 0.962227 | +6 |
| blend, family | 0.006 | 0.962218 | −9.3 (3.6, 1/5) | 0.962237 | +19 |
| blend, family | 0.011 | 0.962216 | −12.1 (3.9, 1/5) | 0.962256 | +40 |

(Δ in 1e-6: plain LR against one-stage plain LR flat C = 0.003, the blends against the version-7 blend.)

- **It was nested-CV optimism.** Built properly, a stack of stacks is no better than one stage and gets
  worse as the penalty weakens, exactly like a single stage on the clean pool. That matches goodpjw2008's
  own measurement in Part 11: Dariush Afshar's stack added to his 245 members lost −14 × 1e-6.
- **The inflation grows with C**, from +1 × 1e-6 at C = 0.003 to about +4 × 1e-5 at C = 0.011. The weaker
  the penalty, the more the stacker leans on a column that has already seen the labels of the fold being
  scored. That is why the curve above never turned, and why the cross-fitted choice and the untouched
  seeds could not catch it: every meta-fold seed scores rows that `gpx_stack245` was fitted on.
- **The public board does not contradict it.** `submission.csv` (C = 0.006) scores **0.96188** and the
  C = 0.011 blend scores **LB_OF_C_0011**, against 0.96187 for the clean 247-member blend and for Dariush
  Afshar's stack. All of them are scored on the same ~20 % of the test set, so the rise from one C to the
  next is one draw seen several times, not independent evidence. The fully nested estimate for the C = 0.011
  blend (−12.1 × 1e-6, SE 3.9, 700k rows) is several times more precise than a public-board difference
  (±1.5 to 2 × 1e-5), and the private board is scored on other rows.
- **What to use**: the clean 247-member blend of version 7 (0.8 LR + 0.2 LightGBM, family C = 0.003),
  validated on three meta-fold seeds without any stack-level member. This version's `submission.csv` is
  valid (its test predictions use nothing they should not), it is just not better than version 7. If you
  load goodpjw2008's extra-members dataset, skip its `stack` files.

def find_dir(slug, root=INPUT, max_depth=6):
    # the mounted folder called `slug` (a dataset, a notebook's output or the competition), or None
    queue, hits = deque([(root, 0)]), []
    while queue:
        d, depth = queue.popleft()
        try:
            entries = sorted(os.scandir(d), key=lambda e: e.name)
        except OSError:
            continue
        for e in entries:
            if e.is_dir():
                if e.name == slug:
                    hits.append(e.path)
                elif depth < max_depth:
                    queue.append((e.path, depth + 1))
    return sorted(hits, key=len)[0] if hits else None

SOURCES = []    # every public source in loading order: its key, the mounted folder's slug, and how to read it


def source(key, slug):
    def register(fn):
        SOURCES.append({"key": key, "slug": slug, "load": fn})
        return fn
    return register


def first(d, name):
    hits = sorted(glob.glob(os.path.join(d, "**", name), recursive=True), key=len)
    if not hits:
        raise FileNotFoundError(name)
    return hits[0]


def by_id(df, ids, col):
    # column `col` of `df` in the order of `ids`; every id has to be there exactly once
    s = df.set_index("id")[col]
    assert s.index.is_unique, "duplicate ids"
    s = s.reindex(ids)
    assert s.notna().all(), f"{int(s.isna().sum()):,} ids missing"
    return s.to_numpy(np.float64)


def npy_pairs(d, prefix, pattern="oof_*.npy"):
    # oof_<name>.npy with test_<name>.npy next to it, rows in train.csv / test.csv order
    for f in sorted(glob.glob(os.path.join(d, "**", pattern), recursive=True)):
        name = os.path.basename(f)[4:-4]
        yield prefix + name, np.load(f), np.load(os.path.join(os.path.dirname(f), f"test_{name}.npy")), "row order"


def npz_pairs(d, prefix, pattern="*.npz", strip=0):
    # one .npz per member holding `oof` and `test`
    for f in sorted(glob.glob(os.path.join(d, "**", pattern), recursive=True)):
        with np.load(f) as z:
            if "oof" in z.files and "test" in z.files:
                yield prefix + os.path.basename(f)[strip:-4], z["oof"], z["test"], "row order"

def logit(p):
    p = np.clip(np.asarray(p, np.float64), 1e-15, 1 - 1e-15)
    return np.log(p / (1 - p))


def is_rank(v):
    # values spread evenly over (0, 1], like rankdata(v) / len(v)
    q = np.quantile(v, [0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9])
    return bool(v.min() >= 0 and v.max() <= 1 and np.abs(q - [0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9]).max() < 0.01)


def corr_cols(Z, r):
    Zc, rc = Z - Z.mean(0), r - r.mean()
    return (Zc * rc[:, None]).sum(0) / np.sqrt((Zc ** 2).sum(0) * (rc ** 2).sum())

# near-copies: rank correlation of the OOF halves, the base first, then the new pairs in loading order
order = list(BASE["col"]) + [int(c) for c in CHK.loc[new & (CHK["status"] == ""), "col"]]
Rk = np.column_stack([rankdata(Zall[:, c]) for c in order])        # float64: the threshold sits in the 5th decimal
Rk -= Rk.mean(0)
Rk /= np.sqrt((Rk ** 2).mean(0))
RHO = np.clip((Rk.T @ Rk) / len(Rk), -1, 1)
del Rk
kept = list(range(len(BASE)))
pos = {c: j for j, c in enumerate(order)}
for j in range(len(BASE), len(order)):
    best = max(kept, key=lambda m: RHO[j, m])
    i = CHK.index[CHK["col"] == order[j]][0]
    CHK.loc[i, "max_rho"] = RHO[j, best]
    if RHO[j, best] > DUP_RHO:
        CHK.loc[i, "status"] = f"near-copy of {CHK.loc[CHK['col'] == order[best], 'member'].iloc[0]} (rho {RHO[j, best]:.6f})"
    else:
        kept.append(j)
FULL_COLS = [order[j] for j in kept]
CHK["in_pool"] = CHK["col"].isin(FULL_COLS)
NAMES = [CHK.loc[CHK["col"] == c, "member"].iloc[0] for c in FULL_COLS]
SRCS = [CHK.loc[CHK["col"] == c, "source"].iloc[0] for c in FULL_COLS]
ZB, ZBT = Zall[:, BASE["col"].to_numpy()], Tall[:, BASE["col"].to_numpy()]
ZF, ZFT = Zall[:, FULL_COLS], Tall[:, FULL_COLS]
N_BASE, N_FULL = ZB.shape[1], ZF.shape[1]
assert len(set(NAMES)) == N_FULL, "duplicate member names"
del Zall, Tall

tab = CHK[CHK["member"] != "(all)"].assign(kept=lambda d: d["in_pool"]).groupby("source", sort=False).agg(
    loaded=("member", "size"), kept=("kept", "sum"), best_oof_auc=("oof_auc", "max"))
tab["dropped"] = tab["loaded"] - tab["kept"]
print(tab[["loaded", "dropped", "kept", "best_oof_auc"]].to_string(formatters={"best_oof_auc": "{:.6f}".format}))
skipped = CHK[CHK["member"] == "(all)"]
if len(skipped):
    print("\nsources not loaded:\n" + skipped[["source", "status"]].to_string(index=False))
drop = CHK[(CHK["member"] != "(all)") & ~CHK["in_pool"] & ~CHK.index.isin(BASE.index)]
reason = drop["status"].str.replace(r" \(.*|:.*| of .*", "", regex=True)
print(f"\nthresholds: OOF AUC from {MIN_AUC} to {MAX_AUC}, agreement gap {AGREE_GAP}, mean drift {DRIFT}, "
      f"near-copy rank correlation above {DUP_RHO}")
print(f"loaded {int((CHK['member'] != '(all)').sum())} pairs from {CHK['source'].nunique()} sources; "
      f"base pool {N_BASE}; new pairs kept {N_FULL - N_BASE}; full pool {N_FULL}")
print("dropped, by reason:", reason.value_counts().to_dict())
print("\nnew pairs that failed a check (near-copies are counted separately below):")
print(drop[~drop["status"].str.startswith("near-copy")][["source", "member", "status"]].to_string(index=False)
      if (~drop["status"].str.startswith("near-copy")).any() else "  none")
print(f"\nnear-copies dropped: {int(drop['status'].str.startswith('near-copy').sum())}")
print(f"\nrank correlation of each kept new pair with its closest pool member: median "
      f"{CHK.loc[CHK['in_pool'] & CHK['max_rho'].notna(), 'max_rho'].median():.5f}, "
      f"lowest {CHK.loc[CHK['in_pool'] & CHK['max_rho'].notna(), 'max_rho'].min():.5f}")
CHK.drop(columns=["col"]).to_csv(f"{WORK}/member_check.csv", index=False)
print(f"every pair's checks and verdict: member_check.csv   [{time.time() - T0:.0f}s]")

## 3. Model families and the LightGBM stacker

The family rule is goodpjw2008's (his loader uses the same `tfm_` prefix for Chris Deotte's set). Scaling a column by s
divides its effective L2 penalty by s². The LightGBM stacker takes the same families as in *Trust the CV*: everything
except Chris Deotte's TFM set, the three newer golem members, Sadam Torres' experiments, Koumei Maki's set and the
Aman Atar / kagankoral encodings members (plus the two sources *Trust the CV* does not load: Aman Atar's forge pairs and
kozykappa's LightGBM).

import lightgbm as lgb
from scipy.stats import spearmanr
Z, ZT = ZF.astype(np.float64), ZFT.astype(np.float64)
del ZF, ZFT, ZB, ZBT
NTR = len(y)

def family(n):
    if 'tabpfn' in n or n.startswith('pfn_'): return 'tabpfn'
    if n.startswith('tfm_') or 'tabicl' in n or 'tabm' in n: return 'tfm'
    if 'realmlp' in n or 'rmlp' in n or 'mlp' in n: return 'mlp'
    return 'gbdt'
FAM = np.array([family(n) for n in NAMES])
SCALE = np.array([{'tabpfn': 3.0, 'tfm': 1.5}.get(f, 1.0) for f in FAM])
print({f: int((FAM == f).sum()) for f in ('gbdt', 'mlp', 'tabpfn', 'tfm')})

GB_EXCLUDE = {'tfm', 'golem', 'sadam', 'koumeimaki', 'kagan_enc', 'aman_v5', 'forge', 'kozy'}
GB_COLS = [i for i, s in enumerate(SRCS) if s not in GB_EXCLUDE]
print(f'{Z.shape[1]} members in the LR stacker, {len(GB_COLS)} in the LightGBM stacker')

def meta_folds(seed):
    mf = np.zeros(NTR, int)
    for k, (_, b) in enumerate(StratifiedKFold(5, shuffle=True, random_state=seed).split(np.zeros(NTR), y)):
        mf[b] = k
    return mf
P = dict(objective='binary', learning_rate=0.03, num_leaves=15, min_data_in_leaf=2000, feature_fraction=0.5,
         bagging_fraction=0.8, bagging_freq=1, lambda_l2=10.0, verbose=-1, num_threads=4, seed=0)
def fit_gb(A, ya):
    ai = np.random.default_rng(0).permutation(len(A)); cut = int(len(ai) * 0.9)
    return lgb.train(P, lgb.Dataset(A[ai[:cut]], ya[ai[:cut]]), 3000, valid_sets=[lgb.Dataset(A[ai[cut:]], ya[ai[cut:]])],
                     callbacks=[lgb.early_stopping(100, verbose=False)])
def nested_gb(mf):
    o = np.zeros(NTR); A = Z[:, GB_COLS]
    for k in range(5):
        a, b = mf != k, mf == k
        m = fit_gb(A[a], y[a]); o[b] = m.predict(A[b], raw_score=True, num_iteration=m.best_iteration)
    return o
zs = lambda v: (v - v.mean()) / v.std()
fa = lambda o, mf: np.array([roc_auc_score(y[mf == k], o[mf == k]) for k in range(5)])

## 4. The grid on meta-fold seed 7

CFG = dict(
    W=0.2,                                    # LightGBM share of the blend (Trust the CV's cross-fitted choice)
    LR_SETTINGS=[('flat', 0.003),('family', 0.01),('family', 0.015),('family', 0.02),('family', 0.025)],
    BASELINE=('lr', 'flat', 0.003),           # this pool's own recipe: plain LR, flat C = 0.003
    SELECT_SEED=42,
    CONFIRM_SEEDS=[7, 101],
    TOL=1e-6, MAX_ITER=20000,                 # a converged solver: 1e-6 differences are then not optimizer noise
)
X = {'flat': (Z, ZT), 'family': (Z * SCALE, ZT * SCALE)}

def _lr(Zm, a, b, C):
    m = LogisticRegression(C=C, tol=CFG['TOL'], max_iter=CFG['MAX_ITER']).fit(Zm[a], y[a])
    return b, m.decision_function(Zm[b])
def nested_lr(Zm, mf, C):
    o = np.zeros(NTR)
    for b, v in Parallel(n_jobs=N_JOBS)(delayed(_lr)(Zm, mf != k, mf == k, C) for k in range(5)):
        o[b] = v
    return o
blend = lambda lr, gb: (1 - CFG['W']) * zs(lr) + CFG['W'] * zs(gb)
def stack_oof(kind, lr, gb):
    return lr if kind == 'lr' else blend(lr, gb)

MF = meta_folds(CFG['SELECT_SEED'])
t = time.time()
GB7 = nested_gb(MF)
print(f"LightGBM stacker alone, seed {CFG['SELECT_SEED']}: fold-mean {fa(GB7, MF).mean():.6f}   ({time.time() - t:.0f}s)", flush=True)
LR7, RES, ROWS = {}, {}, []
for sch, C in CFG['LR_SETTINGS']:
    t = time.time()
    LR7[sch, C] = nested_lr(X[sch][0], MF, C)
    for kind in ('lr', 'blend'):
        f = fa(stack_oof(kind, LR7[sch, C], GB7), MF)
        RES[kind, sch, C] = f
        ROWS.append(dict(stacker=kind, scheme=sch, C=C, seed=CFG['SELECT_SEED'], fold_mean=f.mean(),
                         **{f'fold{k + 1}': v for k, v in enumerate(f)}))
    print(f"{sch:6s} C = {C:<7g} plain LR {RES['lr', sch, C].mean():.6f} | {1 - CFG['W']:.1f} LR + {CFG['W']:.1f} LGB "
          f"{RES['blend', sch, C].mean():.6f}   ({time.time() - t:.0f}s)", flush=True)

## 5. Honest choice and confirmation on untouched seeds

On each confirmation seed the LightGBM stacker is refitted on that seed's meta folds, so every blend there is nested too.

base = RES[CFG['BASELINE']]
keys = list(RES)

## 6. Refit on every training row, write the submission and the OOF

For a blend, the test scores are standardised with each stacker's OOF mean and spread (as in *Trust the CV*), so
`stack_oof.npy` and `stack_test.npy` are on the same scale; for plain LR they are logits. Either way any stacker that
passes values outside [0, 1] through as logits can use the pair as a member.

def refit_lr(sch, C):
    return LogisticRegression(C=C, tol=CFG['TOL'], max_iter=CFG['MAX_ITER']).fit(X[sch][0], y).decision_function(X[sch][1])
sig = lambda v: 1 / (1 + np.exp(-v))
t = time.time()
gb_full = None
def test_scores(kind, sch, C):
    global gb_full
    lr_full = refit_lr(sch, C)
    if kind == 'lr':
        return lr_full, lr_full
    if gb_full is None:
        g = fit_gb(Z[:, GB_COLS], y)
        gb_full = g.predict(ZT[:, GB_COLS], raw_score=True, num_iteration=g.best_iteration)
    lo = LR7[sch, C]
    return (1 - CFG['W']) * (lr_full - lo.mean()) / lo.std() + CFG['W'] * (gb_full - GB7.mean()) / GB7.std(), lr_full
